Subject: Guidelines for the handling applications for GST registration — reg.

It is a bona fide expectation of every person, who applies for GST registration, that his registration
application is quickly processed and GST registration is granted to him without any delay or hassles.
However, grievances have been received regarding the difficulties faced by the applicants in some cases
in getting GST registration, either due to rejection of the application for registration on various grounds, or
due to the delay in processing of the registration application on account of queries being raised/
clarifications or additional documents being sought by the tax officers with respect to information submitted
by the applicant in his application in FORM GST REG-01. While there is a need to exercise due diligence
in processing registration application to prevent GST registration of being obtained by unscrupulous and
fraudulent elements for passing on input tax credit (ITC) without any underlying supply, at the same time, it
is also important to ensure that genuine applicants seeking registration are able to complete the process
without undue inconvenience.

1.2 An analysis of GST registration applications, including the reasons for rejection and the nature of
queries raised during processing, indicates that delays or rejections are often attributable to certain
deficiencies in the applications which may include non-furnishing of requisite information or documents;
submission of incorrect, incomplete, or inconsistent details, or uploading of documents that are not clearly
legible. Such issues are commonly observed in respect of identity details of the applicant, proof of principal
place of business, details relating to the constitution of the business, and identity details of the authorized
signatory.

1.3 It may be relevant to emphasize that proper scrutiny of registration application by the proper officers
plays a critical role in ensuring the integrity of the registration process and in preventing misuse of the

system. Instances have come to notice where some unscrupulous elements have been found to be mis-
utilizing the facility of GST registration by obtaining fake registrations using identity of other persons, either
with or without their knowledge, to fraudulently pass on ineligible input tax credit. Such a malpractice not
only results in huge revenue loss for the Government, but is also against the interest of genuine and
honest taxpayers, making them uncompetitive. To protect the interests of genuine and honest taxpayers,
and to curtail the probability of fake registrations by such unscrupulous elements, the registration process
has been streamlined over a period of time, by amendment in the provisions of CGST Rules, 2017.

1.4 While guidelines for processing of the application for registration have been issued by the Board for the
tax officers vide instruction No. 03/2025-GST dated 17th April 2025, it is felt that similar guidelines may
also be required for the applicants to facilitate them in furnishing proper and correct information and
documents in the application for registration in FORM GST REG-01, so as to reduce requirement of
seeking clarification or additional documents by the tax officers from the applicants during the processing
of the registration applications, and thus expediting the processing of registration applications for genuine
applicants.

2. Accordingly, to ensure uniformity in the implementation of the provisions of the law across field
formations and to further streamline the process of GST registration, the Board, in exercise of its powers
conferred by section 168(1) of the CGST Act, 2017, hereby issues the following guidelines for the
applicants filing application for GST registration.

3. Registration application

3.1 The application for GST registration (in cases other than by a non-resident taxable person, a person
required to deduct tax at source under section 51 of CGST Act, 2017 and a person required to collect tax
under section 52 of CGST Act, 2017 and a person supplying online information and database access or
retrieval services from a place outside India to a non-taxable online recipient in India) is to be filed by the
applicant in FORM GST REG-01 online on the GST portal www.gst.gov.in.

3.1.1 The person required to deduct tax at source under section 51 of CGST Act, 2017 and the
person required to collect tax at source under section 52 of CGST Act, 2017 may apply for GST
registration in FORM GST REG-07 and the non-resident taxable persons need to apply for
registration in FORM GST REG-09. Besides, a person supplying online money gaming from a
place outside India to a person in India or a person supplying online information and database
access or retrieval services from a place outside India to a nontaxable online recipient in India may
apply for GST registration in FORM GST REG-10.

3.1.2 In addition to the guidelines provided in the subsequent paragraphs, a set of Frequently
Asked Questions (FAQs) has also been prepared and annexed herewith as Annexure-I to provide
further practical guidance on commonly encountered issues relating to the filing and processing of
registration applications, including procedural timelines and specific taxpayer scenarios. These
FAQs are intended to minimise ambiguities and facilitate smooth compliance by taxpayers, thereby
reducing the need for repeated references to field officers. Further, a checklist of information and
documents required to be furnished along with the registration application is annexed to this
Circular as Annexure-ll.

3.2 Filing the application for registration

The applicant may access the registration application on the portal by clicking Services>Registration>New
Registration on the home page of GST portal. The detailed manual for step-by-step guidance of the
applicants for filing the registration application has been provided on the GST portal and can be accessed
by clicking Help and Taxpayer Facilities> GST Knowledge Portal> Register with GST from the home page
of the GST portal.

3.2.1. FORM GST REG-01 comprises Part A for generation of Temporary Reference Number
(TRN) and Part B for submission of detailed application, capturing essential fields such as business

3.2.2 Details pertaining to Part A of FORM GST REG-01:

The applicant needs to provide correct details of PAN and the Legal name while filing Part A of
FORM GST REG-01. The Legal Name of the business should be the same as mentioned in PAN
issued by Income Tax Department, and that mentioned in Incorporation Certificate issued by
Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) in case of incorporated companies. In case of proprietorship
concern, the name of the proprietor should be mentioned against the Legal Name. Besides, the
applicant also needs to ensure that correct details of State/ Union Territory and District of his
principal place of business are provided in Part A. The applicant is also advised that in case where
the e-mail address and mobile number are linked to his PAN, the details of the same Email Address
and mobile number may be provided in Part A of FORM GST REG-01

3.3 Details pertaining to Part B of FORM GST REG-01:
3.3.1 Business Details:

In Part B of FORM GST REG-01, the applicant needs to select the correct constitution of the
business, viz. Proprietorship, Partnership, Hindu Undivided Family (HUF), Private Limited
Company, Public Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), Trust, Association of
persons, Local Authority, PSU, etc. Besides, the applicant also needs to upload the copy of
Partnership Deed in case of Partnership firm; Registration Certificate/ Proof of Constitution in case
of Society, Trust, Club, Government Department, Association of Persons or Body of individuals,
Local Authorities, Statutory bodies, etc.; and Karta document in case of HUF as mentioned in
Annexure-ll.

3.3.2 The applicant applying for registration as SEZ Unit or as a SEZ Developer, need to clearly
exercise the option of “Yes” in the correct category and provide the correct name of the said SEZ
unit or the SEZ Developer, as the case may be, and the details of the relevant Approval Order
(Letter of Approval/ Letter of Permission) Number and date of such Approval Order, period of
validity and the designation of the approving authority. Besides, in such cases, the applicant is also
required to upload the copy of such Approval Order.

3.3.3 In cases where the applicant wants to opt for composition scheme at the time for filing
application of registration, he should clearly exercise the option of “Yes” in the option for
composition and also select the correct category for eligibility for composition scheme under the
heading “Category of Registered Parson”. Similarly, in cases where the applicant wants to opt for
Casual Taxable Person and/or Rule 14A, he should clearly exercise the option of “Yes” against the
respective fields.

3.3.4 The applicant is also advised to select the correct reason for obtaining registration from the
drop-down list provided on the portal, such as crossing the threshold; voluntary registration; change
in constitution of business; inter-State supply; liability to pay tax as recipient of goods or services
under section 9(3) or 9(4) of CGST Act, 2017 etc.

3.4. Details of the Promoters/ Partners:

including his/her name, name of the father, date of birth, mobile number, e-mail address, gender,
PAN, Adhaar Number (if available), Director Identification Number (if any), Residential address,
designation/ status in the firm/company etc. In case of partnership firms/ LLP, the details of

person concerned individual and is not part of a group photograph. The photograph should show
the full front face of the concerned person and should be a recent colour photograph of proper
resolution and clarity. Such uploaded photograph should not be a copy of any identity document,
such as PAN, Adhaar Number, Driving License, Passport, etc., either as such or in cropped form.
Due care should be taken while uploading the photographs to ensure that the photograph of the
concerned applicant/ person only is uploaded, as uploading of wrong photograph may lead to
either a query being raised by the tax officer, or the rejection of the registration application.

3.5. Details of Authorized Signatory:

3.5.1 The applicant is required to provide the details of the primary authorized signatory and other
authorized signatories, if any, including the name, name of the father, date of birth, mobile number,
e-mail address, gender, PAN, Adhaar Number, if available, Director Identification Number (DIN),
(where applicable), residential address, designation/ status, etc. The details of maximum ten (10)
authorized signatories can be furnished in the application of the registration. The photographs of
such authorized signatories also need to be uploaded along with the application.

3.5.2 Besides, an authorization letter issued by the proprietor/ Partners/ Board/ Trust/ Management
Committee, etc, appointing the said person as authorised signatory of the entity, must be uploaded
as proof of appointment of the said authorized signatory.

3.5.3 The guidelines regarding the details to be furnished and photographs and documents to be
submitted mentioned in Para 3.4 above are equally applicable in respect of authorized signatories

3.6 Details of Authorized Representative:

3.6.1 The applicant may provide the details of an authorized representative, if any, in the
registration application. Where such authorized representative is a GST Tax Practitioner enrolled
under the provisions of section 48 of CGST Act, 2017, the Enrolment ID of such GST Tax
Practitioner needs to be provided. In case of an authorised representative, other than GST Tax
Practitioner, the details such as name, designation, mobile number, e-mail address, PAN, etc, of
such representative need to be provided.

3.7 Details of Principal Place of Business:

3.7.1 Principal Place of Business (PPoB) is the primary location of business within the State where
registration is applied for. The applicants are advised to be very careful while furnishing the details
of the PPoB in the application for registration, as providing wrong or insufficient or inadequate
details/ documents regarding the PPoB is one of the major reasons for either rejection of the
application for registration, or raising of queries by the tax officers.

3.7.2 The applicant must provide the complete and specific address of PPoB, including but not
limited to, the Building/ Flat/ Shop/ Plot No.; Khasra/ Khata Number, where applicable; Road/
Street; Locality/ Sub-locality; Village/ Town/ City; Taluka/ Block, where applicable; District; PIN
Code; and nearby landmarks, if any. Due care should be taken to ensure that the correct PIN Code
is mentioned in the said details, and as far as possible, latitude and longitude of the PPoB may also
be provided. It is mentioned that the correct longitude and latitude of the PPoB can also be
selected by the applicant on the portal while filing the details in the said application by dragging the
marker on the map on the portal to the correct address of the PPoB. It is again reiterated that the
address provided should be very specific and accurate for the said PPoB, to the extent of the
particular shop/ house/ premises/ Flat, etc. and should not be left open, such as mentioning only
the details of Khasra Number/ road/ locality/ colony/ village/ town, etc. Not providing the specific
and accurate address of PPoB may result in issuance of queries by the tax officers or negative
report in physical verification report of the PPoB by the field officer, wherever applicable. The
address furnished in the application form should match the address mentioned in the document
detailed in para 3.8 furnished and uploaded with the application, as proof of Principal Place of
Business.

3.7.3 Jurisdiction Details:

The applicant also needs to select the correct State as well as Centre jurisdiction from the drop-
down menu shown on the portal as per the PIN Code details provided by the applicant. This will
avoid any delay in processing of application of registration due to the requirement of transferring
the application of registration or reassigning the ARN for physical verification to the correct
jurisdictional tax authorities. Besides, correct contact details in form of e-mail address and mobile
number need to be provided. Nature of the business to be carried out from the said premises, such
as manufacturing/ wholesale business/ retail business/ supplier of services/ warehouse/ Depot, etc.
should also be mentioned by the applicant from the drop-down menu provided on the portal.

3.8 Documents to be furnished in respect of Principal Place of Business (PPOB):

3.8.1 A list of requisite documents to be uploaded as proof of the Principal Place of Business while
filing the registration application has been appended with FORM GST REG-01 on the portal and is
also annexed to this Circular as Annexure-lIl. The documentary requirements in respect of
different types of possession of the said premises are explained in detail in the subsequent
paragraphs:

3.8.2 Own premises:

3.8.2.1 The premises will be considered as owned by the applicant, if he himself, is the
owner of the said premises. If the premises is not in the name of the applicant, but is in the
name of some of the family members like parents/ spouse/ siblings, etc. or in the name of
one of the partners/ directors, etc., then the said premises cannot be considered to be
owned by the said entity/ applicant. In such cases, the applicant should not select “own” as
the option under the “Nature of Premises”, and instead, should select “Consent” or
“rented/leased” or “shared”, as the case may be, in respect of the nature of the said
premises.

3.8.2.2 In cases where the premises for PPoB is in name of the applicant and is owned by
the applicant himself, the applicant needs to upload a clear and legible copy of the
document as proof of the premises owned by him. In this regard, the applicant may submit
the copy of latest Property Tax receipt or copy of Municipal Khata or copy of document
certifying Property Identification or latest copy of Electricity Bill or latest copy of
Water Bill for the said premises in the name of the applicant and showing full and
complete address of the said premises, which should be same as mentioned by the
applicant in the application of registration.

3.8.2.3 In case the premises is owned by the deceased ancestor of the applicant and
no document is available in the name of the applicant, then an affidavit in the
requisite format annexed as Annexure — IV needs to be uploaded by the applicant to this
effect alongwith a clear and legible copy of death certificate of deceased ancestor and any
one of the documents mentioned above in Para 3.8.2.2 in the name of the deceased
ancestor or in the applicant’s name for the premises corresponding to the address to be
declared as PPoB/APoB.

3.8.3 Rented or leased premises:
(a) Where lease/ rent agreement is available:

i. In respect of premises which are taken on rent or lease by the applicant and
registered rent/lease agreement is available, the applicant is required to upload a
clear and legible copy of the valid registered Rent/Lease agreement. In case where
the rent/ lease deed is required to be mandatorily registered as per provision of
laws/regulations of some States/Region concerned, either based on usage of the
rented/ leased property or where the rent/ lease is for a period exceeding the
specified period, and therefore, the applicant needs to upload a clear and legible
copy of valid registered rent/ lease agreement in such cases.

ii. In cases, where rent / lease agreement is not required to be registered, in
such cases, the applicant will have to upload a clear and legible copy of rent/lease
agreement alongwith any one of the documents, as mentioned in Para 3.8.2 above
in the name of the landlord/lessor corresponding to the address to be declared as
PPoB.

iii. In cases where the electricity or water connection for the said rented/ leased
premises is in the name of the applicant tenant, the applicant will have to furnish
a clear and legible copy of the latest electricity bill or water bill issued in his name,
clearly indicating the complete address of the said premises, along with a valid rent
agreement. Such documents may be accepted in lieu of the proof of premises
required from the lessor.

(b) Where lease/ rent agreement is not available:

In respect of rented/ leased premises, where the rent/ lease agreement is not
available, an undertaking in the requisite format annexed as Annexure V needs to

3.8.4 Premises on consent basis:

3.8.4.1 Where the ownership of the premises is with a family member like spouse, parents,
sibling, etc. or with a partner/ director, etc. and premises is made available to the applicant
on consent basis, the applicant needs to upload consent letter in the requisite format
annexed as Annexure VI issued in plain paper by the concerned owner of the premises
duly signed by him and a clear and legible copy of any one of the documents as mentioned
in Para 3.8.2 above in the name of the consenter corresponding to the address to be
declared as PPoB. The consent letter should clearly mention the details of the consenter
and the applicant, the details of the property/ premises in respect of which consent is being
given and the relationship between the consenter and the applicant. In cases, where the
premises is co-owned by more than one owner, consent of all such co-owners needs to be
provided.

3.8.5 Co-working Spaces:

In case where the applicant has declared co-working space as PPoB, the applicant needs to
upload a clear and legible copy of the membership agreement with the co-working space provider,
specifically providing details of the period of the agreement, specific suite/ cabin/ desk number
allocated to the applicant, details as to whether the said premises is owned by the co-working
space provider or whether the said premises has been taken on rent/ lease by the co-working
space provider from the property owner. In case the said premises is owned by the co-working
space provider, the applicant needs to also upload the copy of a document as mentioned in Para
3.8.2.2 above in the name of the co-working space provider corresponding to the address to be
declared as PPoB. In case where the said premises has been taken on rent/ lease by the co-
working space provider from the property owner, the applicant needs to upload the copy of the valid
registered lease/ rent agreement entered between the co-working space provider and the property
owner in addition to the membership agreement with the co-working space provider

3.8.6 SEZ/ SEZ Developer:

If the principal place of business is located in the Special Economic Zone or the applicant is a
Special Economic Zone developer, a clear and legible copy of approval letter (Letter of Approval/
Letter of Permission), with due validity, issued by the Development Commissioner of the SEZ is
required to be uploaded.

3.8.7 It is reiterated that while uploading any such documents on the portal as a part of application
for registration, due care must be exercised by the applicant to ensure that such uploaded
documents are clear, complete and fully legible

3.9 Details of Additional Place of Business:

In cases where the applicant needs to provide details of any additional place of business, the details of all
such additional places of business in the said State of registration may be provided. It is further clarified
that the documentary requirements for amending details of the Principal Place of Business or Additional
Place of Business remain same as those specified for the Principal Place of Business in the preceding
paragraphs and as detailed in the appended list (Annexure Ill). It is also clarified that telecommunication
towers, bank Automated Teller Machine (ATM), which are unmanned installations are not required to be
declared as the PPOB or as an APOB in the GST registration application.

In some States, the applicant is also required to provide some State specific information in the application

3.11 Details of the Bank Account:

As per rule 10A of CGST Rules, 2017, a registered person is required to furnish the details of the bank
accounts, which are in the name of the applicant/ registered person and have been obtained on the PAN of
the applicant/ registered person, within a period of 30 days from the date of grant of registration, or before
furnishing of details of outward supplies in FORM GSTR-1/ Invoice Furnishing Facility, whichever is earlier.
Accordingly, whereas furnishing of the details of the bank account is not mandatory at the time of filing
application for registration, the applicant is advised to furnish the details of his Bank Account, where
available at the time of applying for registration, in his application for registration. Such details include the
Account Number, type of account, Bank Name, IFSC Code, Branch Address. The said Bank account
should invariably be in the name of the applicant and should be obtained on the PAN of the applicant. The
applicant can give details of more than one account in the application for registration. The applicant is also
required to upload the documents related to the said bank account(s) in form of scanned copy of first page
of the bank passbook, or the relevant page of the Bank Statement, or the scanned copy of the cancelled
cheque containing the name of the Proprietor/ Business entity, Bank Account No., IFSC and Bank Branch
details, etc.

4. In cases, where the application for registration is being filed by a person whose previous registration,
obtained on the same PAN, had been suspended as per the provisions of sub-rule (2) or sub-rule (2A) of
rule 21A of CGST Rules, 2017, or had been cancelled as per the provisions of rule 22 of CGST Rules,
2017, the applicant needs to ensure that all his due returns for such suo-moto suspended or cancelled
registration, including final return in FORM GSTR-10, as applicable, have been filed and no liability is
pending against the said suo-moto suspended/ cancelled registration.

5. Processing of registration application:

5.1 As per provisions of rule 9 of CGST Rules, 2017 in cases, where the applicant has opted for
authentication of Adhaar number, as specified in sub-rule (4A) of rule 8 of CGST Rules, 2017, while filing
the application for registration, and has successfully got the Adhaar authenticated, the registration
application is required to be processed by the proper officer within a period of seven working days from the
date of submission of the application. In cases, where the applicant has either not opted for authentication
of Adhaar number or fails to undergo such authentication of Adhaar number; or where the applicant is
identified on the common portal, based on data analysis and risk parameters, for carrying out physical
verification of places of business; or where the proper officer, with the approval of competent authority,
deems it fit to carry out physical verification of places of business, the registration application is to be
processed within thirty days of the submission of the application for registration, after physical verification
of the place of business according to the provisions of rule 25 of CGST Rules. It is, therefore, in the
interest of the applicant that he opts for and undergoes authentication of Adhaar number, as specified in
sub-rule (4A) of rule 8 of CGST Rules, 2017, to get his application for registration processed at the earliest.

5.2 It is also added that as per first and second provisos to sub-rule (4A) of rule 8 of CGST Rules, 2017, in
cases where the applicant has opted for authentication of Adhaar number, the applicant may be identified
on the common portal, based on data analysis and risk parameters, for biometric based Adhaar
authentication, for taking photograph of the applicant, and for verification of original copies of the
documents uploaded by the applicant in the application for registration, at one of the Facilitation Centres
notified for the said purpose. In cases, where the applicant has not opted for authentication of Adhaar
number, he will be required to visit the Facilitation Centre for the purpose of taking the photograph and for

verification of original copies of the documents uploaded by him in the application for registration. In such
cases, application for registration shall be deemed to be complete only on successful verification as per

5.3 Small taxpayers who mainly supply to unregistered persons, or issue tax invoices in respect of supplies
made to registered persons for passing on ITC on a monthly basis below Rs. 2.5 lakh, may take the benefit
of simplified registration under Rule 14A of the CGST Rules, 2017. They can opt to pass on ITC below Rs.
2.5 lakh per month while applying for GST registration under FORM GST REG-01. Under Rule 14A,
registration is granted automatically by the system within 3 working days. Similarly, applicants identified by
the system based on risk-based parameters and data analytics for electronic grant of registration under
Rule 9A are also given registration automatically by the system within 3 working days from the date of
submission of the application.

5.4 Where the application is found to be deficient, either because of incomplete or contradictory details or
information or documents furnished by the applicant; or due to the documents being illegible, not clear or
not providing complete details, such as identity of the person/ complete address, etc., the proper officer
may seek a Clarification or information or documents through a notice in FORM GST REG-03, within seven
working days from the date of submission of application only in the following cases:

i. Where any document is incomplete or not legible, the proper officer may seek complete or
legible copy of the same.

ii. Where the address of place of business does not match with the document uploaded by
the applicant, or where such uploaded document does not appear to be a valid proof of the
address of the said place of business, the proper officer may seek additional documents as
per the list of documents in Annexure-IV to confirm the address details.

iii. Where the address of place of business is incomplete or vague, the proper officer may
seek complete and unambiguous details of the address along with the corresponding
documentary proof.

5.4.1 It has also been observed that sometimes unwarranted documents are sought by raising
queries such as residential address of the applicant/Managing Director/Authorized Signatory not
being in the same city or the State where the registration has been sought; Udyam Certificate;
raising objections that the kind of activities mentioned in the registration application cannot be
conducted from the particular premises etc. While raising queries/seeking additional documents,
officers handling registration applications should not seek unwarranted documents or raise any
presumptive query which is not relevant for processing of registration application.

5.4.2 The proper officer may issue a notice electronically to the applicant in FORM GST REG-03,
as referred to in para 5.4 above, in cases where the application has not been identified by the
system for physical verification of the place of business. In cases where the application has been
identified by the system for physical verification, or where the proper officer, with the approval of an
officer authorised by the Commissioner not below the rank of Assistant Commissioner, considers
physical verification of the place of business necessary, such notice shall be issued within thirty
days from the date of submission of the application.

5.5 The applicant is required to submit the said clarification, information or the documents in FORM
GST REG-04 within a period of seven working days from the date of receipt of such notice. The
proper officer is, thereafter, required to take a decision of the application of registration within seven

Q.8. Is there any simplified procedure for obtaining GST registration for small businesses.

A. Yes, a simplified registration scheme for small businesses has been introduced with effect from 1st
November 2025 as per rule 14A of Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017 (CGST Rules) 2017. This
optional scheme is for small businesses who do not need to pass on Input Tax Credit
(IGST+CGST+SGST) more than %2.5 lakhs per month to registered recipients. Under this scheme,
registration is granted automatically within three working days from the date of application without any
intervention by officer. It may however be noted that simplified registration scheme under rule 14A of CGST
Rules, 2017 is not applicable in cases where the person needs to pass on ITC more than Rs. 2.5 lakh ina
month.

Q.9. What are the conditions and restriction for obtaining simplified GST registration?
A. To avail this simplified scheme, a small supplier must:

i. file application for GST registration in FORM GST REG-01,

ii. opt for Aadhaar authentication while filing the application and

iii. choose registration under rule 14A by declaring that they will abide by all conditions and
restrictions as mentioned under:

a. He cannot declare outward taxable supplies to registered persons involving total tax
amount exceeding Rs.2.5 lakh in a month.

b. He cannot obtain more than one GST registration in a State/Union Territory on the same
PAN under Rule 14A of CGST Rules, 2017.

following manner:

File all pending returns due till the date of filing of application for withdrawal in FORM GST REG-32
on the common portal, subject to condition that you should have filed at least return for one tax
period after obtaining registration under rule 14A of CGST Rules, 2017.

Once you submit FORM GST REG-32, your withdrawal application will be processed using the same
procedure as a normal registration application made under FORM GST REG-01. After approval, you will
become a regular taxpayer and will no longer be subject to the Rule 14A restrictions.
