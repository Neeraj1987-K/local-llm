Central Goods and Services Tax (Amendment) Rules, 2026. - 05/2026 - Central GST (CGST)
MINISTRY OF FINANCE
(Department of Revenue)
(CENTRAL BOARD OF INDIRECT TAXES AND CUSTOMS)
NOTIFICATION NO. 05/2026- CENTRAL TAX
New Delhi, the 26th October, 2026

G.S.R. 919(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 164 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (12 of 2017), the Central Government on
the recommendations of the Council, hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017, namely: —

1. Short title and Commencement. —
(1) These rules may be called the Central Goods and Services Tax (Amendment) Rules, 2026.
(2) Save as otherwise provided in these rules, they shall come into force on the 1st day of November,2026.

2. In the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017 (hereinafter referred to as the principal rules), after rule 14A, the following rule shall be inserted, namely: -

“14B, Option for suppliers supplying goods through electronic commerce operator who are required to collect tax at source under section 52—
(1) A person registered in a State or Union territory under rule (9) may obtain registration against the same Permanent Account Number in any other State
or Union territory where the person has no physical presence, by declaring a warehouse, of an electronic commerce operator, who is required to collect
tax at source under section 52 of the Act, in that State or Union territory as principal place of business, subject to the following conditions, namely: —

(d) the person intends to make supply of goods through electronic commerce operator who are required to collect tax at source under section 52 of
the Act;

(e) the person determines that the total output tax liability on such supplies to registered persons comprising Central tax and State tax or Union
territory tax and integrated tax other than a person registered on the same Permanent Account Number, does not exceed two lakh fifty thousand
rupees per month;

(f) the electronic commerce operator consents to the registered person for declaring the warehouse as the principal place of business.

(2) A person who intends to obtain registration under sub-rule (1) in a State or Union territory shall apply in FORM GST REG-01 and declare the Goods
and Services Tax Identification Number obtained on his Permanent Account Number in any State or Union territory (hereinafter referred to as the ‘home
State’) where the person has a physical presence.

(3) The applicant shall furnish the address and particulars of the warehouse of the electronic commerce operator in the State or Union territory where the
person intends to obtain registration under sub-rule (1) as a principal place of business and the address and particulars of any other warehouse, in that
State or Union territory, of the same or any other electronic commerce operator as an additional place of business.

(4) The application under sub-rule (2) shall be forwarded to the concemed electronic commerce operator for communicating their consent to the
declaration of the warehouse as principal place of business or additional place of business, as the case may be.

(5) The electronic commerce operator may consent within seven working days and where consent is not given within that period, the application shall not
be processed further.

(6) On receipt of consent from the electronic commerce operator, the application shall be forwarded to the jurisdictional officer of the home State for
biometric-based Aadhaar authentication and verification of documents, if not done successfully earlier.

(7) On successful completion of the authentication and verification, the officer shall proceed to the physical verification of the principal place of business in
the home State, if not done successfully earlier.

(8) On successful completion of physical verification, registration shall be granted electronically.

(9) Where the applicant fails to complete the biometric-based Aadhaar authentication and verification of documents, the application shall not be processed
further.

(10) Where on physical verification, it is found that the applicant does not conduct any business in the home State:
(a) the application shall be deemed to be rejected;
(b) the rejection shall be intimated electronically in FORM GST REG-05A.

(11) Where the proper officer fails to carry out the physical verification within thirty days from the date of generation of the application reference number
after successful completion of biometric-based Aadhaar authentication and document verification, the application shall be deemed to be approved

(12) Where registration has been granted for the principal place of business in the home State after successful completion of biometric based Aadhar
Authentication, document verification and physical verification of the premises, the registration shall be granted electronically upon receipt of consent from
the electronic commerce operator.

(13) The provision of grant of registration under rule 10 shall, mutatis mutandis, apply to the application submitted under this rule.

(14) The certificate of registration shall contain the details of the warehouse of electronic commerce operator declared as principal place of business and
additional place of business if any, the address of principal place of business in the home State where the registered person has a physical presence.

(15) A person who has obtained registration in the home State under rule 14A and intends to obtain registration under sub-rule (1), shall before submitting
an application under this rule, submit an application in FORM GST REG-32 to withdraw from the option availed under rule 14.
