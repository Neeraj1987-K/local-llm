New Delhi, the 28th October, 2017
Order No. 06/2017-GST
Subject: Extension of time limit for submitting application in FORM GST REG-26

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-rule (2) of rule 24 of the Central Goods and
Services Tax Rules, 2017 read with section 168 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, the
Commissioner, on the recommendations of the Council, hereby extends the period for submitting
electronicallythe application in the FORM GST REG- 26 till 31st December 2017.

(Upender Gupta)

Commissioner (GST)
