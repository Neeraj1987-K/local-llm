MINISTRY OF FINANCE

NOTIFICATION NO. 03/2022-Central Tax
New Delhi, the 31st March, 2022

G.S.R. 242(E).-In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 23 of the Central Goods
and Services Tax Act, 2017 (12 of 2017), the Central Government, on the recommendations of the Council,

Finance (Department of Revenue), _No.10/2019-Central Tax, dated the 7th March, 2019, published in the
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part Il, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide number G.S.R. 190(E), dated the 7th
March, 2019, namely:-

In the said notification, in the Table, after serial number 3 and the entries relating thereto, the following
